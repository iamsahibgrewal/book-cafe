§ 02 Chapter One

We couldn't choose between a café and a library, so we built both.

Most cafés want your table back. Most libraries want your coffee outside. We wanted a room where you could have both for as long as you liked, so every wall at Book Cafe is a bookshelf and every shelf is a lending library.

The books come from neighbours, publishers and our own collection. You can borrow any of them with any drink, return them whenever you're done, and there are never any late fees.

§ 06 Lending Rules

How borrowing works, in five steps.

No sign-up forms or deposits. If you've bought a drink today, you can take a book home.

  1. Step 1

    Order anything at the counter

    An espresso, a pot of tea or a slice of cake. Any order counts as your library pass for the day.

  2. Step 2

    Pick a book from any shelf

    Every book in the building is part of the library, except the ones in the glass case by the door.

  3. Step 3

    Get it stamped

    Bring it to the counter. We stamp the due date inside the back cover, three weeks from today.

  4. Step 4

    Read here, or take it home

    Stay at your table as long as you like, or take it with you. Drip refills are free while you read.

  5. Step 5

    Return it, or swap it

    Drop it in the return slot by the door. Late? No fines. Leave one of your own books and you can keep ours.

§ 07 Reading Room Etiquette

Three house rules, politely kept.

I.

The Long Room is for quiet.

Talk all you like in the main hall and the Garden Room. Upstairs in the Long Room, it's pages turning and cups set down softly.

II.

Stay as long as the chapter.

No time limits and no laptop curfews. If the place is full, we may ask you to share a table, never to leave.

III.

Leave a note, not a mark.

Please don't write in the margins. Use one of the bookmark cards at the counter to leave a note for the next reader.
